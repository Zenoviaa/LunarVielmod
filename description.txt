[h2]What is Lunar Veil 2.0?[/h2]

[b]
Lunar veil 2.0 is a super expansive and super invasive, finished/fully completeable mod that utilizes dodging and neat mechanics to spice up the gameplay of terraria! 
This mod focuses on adding new content in ways that are unique and different from other mods, completely reworking parts of the game as to fit the story 
constructed for you!
[/b]


[u]
Note:
This mod is multiplayer compatible,very difficult, changes the progression and gameplay a lot as well as being very active, there is a lot of changes to the way you may play the game,
so you can almost look at this mod as a wholely new experience almost. We would reccommend playing Expertmode first if you want the normal experience.
[/u]

[i]
The mod totals a whopping 50+ bosses, including 5 secret bosses
300+ Weapons, 50+ Town or Quest Npcs, etc. blah blah too much in this mod to explain.
So so many new biomes, including overhauls to the vanilla ones, structures, and decorations!
And a story crafted to guide you along the game mixed with the Lunar Veil's story!
[/i]
